SHEMART ONLINE STORE — QUICK START

1. Open index.html in a modern browser to preview the home page. The site is split into index.html (home), shop.html (catalogue), about.html (brand story), and contact.html (customer care).
2. Products, prices, descriptions and image links are sample data. Edit the productsData array inside script.js to add your real products.
3. Find: const whatsappNumber='923001234567'; Replace it with your WhatsApp number in international format, digits only. Example format for Pakistan: 92 followed by your mobile number without the first 0.
4. Replace footer contact details with your real business WhatsApp and email.
5. This is a front-end demo. The cart and checkout work in the browser, and an order summary can be prepared. It does NOT save orders to a database or send orders to an admin automatically. After adding your number, the WhatsApp order button opens a WhatsApp message.
6. To publish publicly, upload all HTML files, styles.css, and script.js to a static hosting service or connect a domain. For real order management, inventory, customer accounts and payment gateway, connect a backend or use an e-commerce platform such as Shopify/WooCommerce.
7. Prices are illustrative PKR sample prices; verify all product names, prices, stock, delivery charges, and policies before launch.
